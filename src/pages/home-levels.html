The guest wall

A shared pixel canvas for everyone who visits this site. Instead of signing a guest book, paint something: a doodle, your initials, a tiny piece of pixel art. It's live, so you'll see other visitors' strokes appear as they paint.

How to paint
Pick a colour and a brush size, then click or drag on the canvas
Shared and live
One canvas for every visitor, updating in real time
It stays
Your pixels last until someone paints over them
Keyboard and phone
Arrow keys and Enter to paint; on a phone, switch to Move to scroll

Live canvas · 160 × 60
