S'mores by the fire

You made it past the castle. Some campers are gathered around the fire, and they're hungry.

  1. Pick an order. Each camper wants their marshmallow toasted a certain way, on a certain cracker, with a certain chocolate.
  2. Roast. Hold it in the flames and turn it so both sides cook evenly. If it catches fire, blow it out fast (unless they like it burnt).
  3. Build and serve. Cracker, chocolate, marshmallow, cracker. Serve it before they get bored and wander off.

Keys: Space roast · T turn · B blow · 1–3 pick camper · ↵ serve
