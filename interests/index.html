Hiking

Trails, switchbacks and whatever view is waiting at the top.

Hiking is how I hit reset. There's something about a trailhead that makes the rest of the week feel far away: the climb, the switchbacks, and then the moment the trees open up and the whole valley is laid out below. Around Berkeley that means the East Bay hills and regional parks, and further out, any trail that ends somewhere worth the climb.

Racket sports

If there's a racket, a net and a rally, I'm in.

I'll play anything with a racket and a net: tennis, badminton, table tennis, pickleball. Each one has its own rhythm, but they all reward the same things: quick feet, good anticipation and the patience to keep a rally going. Most of all, they're an easy way to turn an afternoon with friends into a friendly rivalry.
