Epic · AI

No Limit Poker Bot

Python · Decision trees · Monte Carlo

A bot for No Limit, the student poker-bot tournament run by Sequoia, OpenAI and Citadel Securities. Three interpretable decision trees (stack vs. table average, hand strength, opponent modeling) feed an equity and EV step, with thresholds tuned by simulating the scored tournaments. It finished 1st on an 8-bot ladder and averages +10 points per table against a tight-aggressive baseline, with every decision under 1 ms. The code is private; the overview covers the model and results.

Rare · ML

Fintrax

Python · PyTorch · Hugging Face · Beautiful Soup

Scores how confident management sounds on earnings calls and turns it into BUY/HOLD/SELL signals. A FinBERT model, fine-tuned on weak labels from the Loughran-McDonald word lists plus hedge and certainty phrases, rates each sentence; call scores are z-scored against all earlier calls and backtested against SPY. Transcripts are scraped from the Motley Fool.
